# 🎯 HRMS Module - Complete Features List

## ✅ Currently Implemented Features

### 1. 👥 Employee Management
- ✅ Create Employee Profile
- ✅ View All Employees
- ✅ View Employee Details
- ✅ Update Employee Information
- ✅ Update Employee Status (Active/Inactive/Resigned/Terminated)
- ✅ Employee Code Auto-generation
- ✅ Department Assignment
- ✅ Designation Tracking
- ✅ Employment Type (Full-Time, Part-Time, Contract, Intern)
- ✅ Personal Details (DOB, Gender, Blood Group, Address)
- ✅ Emergency Contact Information
- ✅ Bank Details (Account, IFSC, PAN, Aadhar)

### 2. 📅 Leave Management
- ✅ Apply Leave
- ✅ View All Leaves
- ✅ Approve/Reject Leave
- ✅ Leave Balance Tracking (Auto-created on employee creation)
- ✅ Multiple Leave Types:
  - Sick Leave (12 days/year)
  - Casual Leave (12 days/year)
  - Earned Leave (15 days/year)
  - Maternity Leave
  - Paternity Leave
  - Unpaid Leave
- ✅ Auto-calculate Leave Days
- ✅ Leave History
- ✅ Leave Balance Auto-update on Approval

### 3. 📊 Performance Management
- ✅ Create Performance Review
- ✅ View All Reviews
- ✅ Update Review Status
- ✅ Multi-criteria Evaluation:
  - Technical Skills (1-5)
  - Communication (1-5)
  - Teamwork (1-5)
  - Punctuality (1-5)
  - Quality of Work (1-5)
- ✅ Auto-calculate Overall Rating
- ✅ Strengths & Areas of Improvement
- ✅ Goal Setting
- ✅ Comments & Feedback
- ✅ Reviewer Tracking

### 4. 📄 Document Management
- ✅ Upload Employee Documents
- ✅ View Documents by Employee
- ✅ Delete Documents
- ✅ Document Types:
  - Resume
  - ID Proof
  - Address Proof
  - Education Certificates
  - Experience Letters
  - Other Documents
- ✅ Upload Tracking (Who uploaded, When)

### 5. 🏢 Department Management
- ✅ Create Department
- ✅ View All Departments
- ✅ Active/Inactive Status
- ✅ Department-wise Employee Grouping
